CHAPTER 01

LIMITS

The mathematics of approaching a value.

lim x → a f(x) = L

THE CENTRAL IDEA

What is a limit?

A limit describes the value that a function approaches as its input gets closer and closer to a particular value.

x → a
→
f(x) → L

FIRST EXAMPLE

Evaluate the limit

lim x → 2 (x² − 4)/(x − 2)

Step 1: Factorise the numerator.

x² − 4 = (x − 2)(x + 2)

Step 2: Cancel the common factor.

(x − 2)(x + 2)/(x − 2) = x + 2

Step 3: Now take the limit.

∴   lim x → 2 (x + 2) = 4
