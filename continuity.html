CHAPTER 03

CONTINUITY

When a function has no break, jump or hole at a point.

lim x → a f(x) = f(a)

THE CENTRAL IDEA

What does continuity mean?

A function is continuous at x = a when three conditions are satisfied:

1.   f(a) exists
2.   lim x → a f(x) exists
3.   lim x → a f(x) = f(a)

A SIMPLE EXAMPLE

Is this function continuous?

f(x) = x²

Consider x = 2.

f(2) = 4
lim x → 2 x² = 4
∴   lim x → 2 f(x) = f(2)

Therefore, f(x) = x² is continuous at x = 2.

WHEN CONTINUITY FAILS

Types of Discontinuity

01

Removable

A hole occurs at a point, but the surrounding function approaches a definite value.

02

Jump

The left-hand and right-hand limits approach different values.

03

Infinite

The function grows without bound near a particular point.

04

Continuity & Derivatives

Every differentiable function is continuous, but not every continuous function is differentiable.
