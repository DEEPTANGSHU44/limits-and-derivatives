CALCULUS IN THE REAL WORLD

APPLICATIONS
OF CALCULUS

Calculus is the mathematics of change. From motion to physics, engineering to economics, derivatives allow us to describe how quantities change.

01 · MOTION

Position → Velocity → Acceleration

If the position of an object is known as a function of time, derivatives reveal its instantaneous velocity and acceleration.

POSITION s(t)
→
VELOCITY v(t) = s′(t)
→
ACCELERATION a(t) = v′(t) = s″(t)

Example

s(t) = t² + 3t
v(t) = s′(t) = 2t + 3
a(t) = v′(t) = 2

02 · OPTIMIZATION

Finding the Best Possible Value

Derivatives help locate maximum and minimum values. This is the mathematical foundation of optimization.

PROBLEM

Find the dimensions of a rectangle with perimeter 20 units that produces the maximum possible area.

CALCULUS

2x + 2y = 20
y = 10 - x
A(x) = x(10-x)
A′(x) = 10 - 2x
A′(x)=0
x = 5

Therefore the maximum-area rectangle is a 5 × 5 square.

03 · PHYSICS

The Language of Change

Many fundamental quantities in physics are related through derivatives.

Motion

v = ds/dt

Velocity is the rate of change of position.

Acceleration

a = dv/dt

Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity.

Electricity

I = dQ/dt

Electric current describes the rate at which electric charge flows.

Wave Motion

∂²ψ/∂t²

Differential equations describe how waves evolve through space and time.
